**{draw(Modernity)}**

2026

One-time live sound performance set

21’41 min

Indoor performance with a projection and a set of speakers

Sound: Farhanaz Rupaidha, Chufan Luo 

Visual: Chufan Luo 


* * * * *

Merupakan sebuah projek kolaborasi berupa _live sound performance set_ dengan Chufan Luo yang pertama kali dipertunjukan pada pameran Waiting Room di fffriedrich, Frankfurt am Main.

_Performance_ berdurasi selama 21 menit 41 detik ini berlangsung pada pertengahan durasi pameran pada tanggal 13 September 2026. Pameran Waiting Room sendiri berlangsung dari tanggal 4 hingga 20 September 2026.

Sebagai bagian dari eksperimentasi konsep pameran yang semula bersifat ‘sunyi’, _performance_ **{draw(Modernity)}** menghidupkan ruang pamer dengan ragam bunyi yang dipertunjukan ulang melalui dokumentasi video lengkap dengan pencahayaan yang diatur menyesuaikan pertumbuhan yang terjadi pada ruang pamer.

------

A collaborative project in the form of a _live sound performance set_, created in collaboration with Chufan Luo, which was first presented as part of the exhibition _Waiting Room_ at fffriedrich, Frankfurt am Main.

The 21-minute-and-41-second performance took place midway through the exhibition, on 13 September 2026. The exhibition _Waiting Room_ ran from 4 to 20 September 2026.

As part of an experiment with the exhibition's initially ‘silent’ concept, the performance **{draw(Modernity)}** activated the exhibition space through a range of sounds, which were subsequently presented again through a video documentation of the full performance, accompanied by lighting adjusted in response to the growth taking place within the exhibition space.
